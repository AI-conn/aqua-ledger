AquaLedger

See your water use. Change one habit.

A small tool for SDG 6

AquaLedger is a free, offline-first, single-page tracker for personal water use. Log litres by category, see a 7-day trend, and track a daily target you choose yourself. No account, no server, no tracking — your data stays in this browser.

The default target (100 L/day) is an editable planning number, not an official standard. Set your own based on your context.
